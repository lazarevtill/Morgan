## Research Skill

Structured approach to finding and synthesising information.

1. Clarify the question — restate it to confirm scope before searching.
2. Break it into 2-3 sub-questions.
3. For each sub-question: search → read → extract relevant facts.
4. Cross-check claims across at least two independent sources where possible.
5. Cite every source with URL and access date.
6. Summarise findings in a clear, structured response; flag uncertainty explicitly.
