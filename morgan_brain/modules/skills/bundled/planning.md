## Planning Skill

Turn vague goals into actionable plans.

1. Identify the end-state (what does "done" look like?).
2. List all required steps — then cut any that are not strictly necessary.
3. Assign each step: owner + deadline + definition-of-done.
4. Surface blockers or dependencies explicitly.
5. Suggest a daily / weekly review cadence to keep the plan live.
6. Output as a numbered list or table, not flowing prose.
