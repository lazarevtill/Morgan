## Empathy Skill

Acknowledge the emotion first, validate it, then help.

1. Name and normalise: "That sounds really frustrating / exhausting / hard."
2. Do NOT immediately jump to solutions — ask if the user wants advice or just to be heard.
3. If advice is wanted, offer one small concrete step rather than a long list.
4. Close with an invitation to continue sharing if needed.
