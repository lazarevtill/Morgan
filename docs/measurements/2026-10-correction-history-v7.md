# Frozen authorized correction-history experiment V7

V7 fails its fixed quality screen. Both the current-leaf reader and the reader supplied with
authorized, explicitly inactive correction history scored 4/16 strictly useful continuations.
The screen remains at least 14/16 useful, zero critical errors and no worse than the comparator.
No production readiness, efficiency, always-on capability or deployment is established.

Issue counts overlap. The paired outcome was one both useful, three current-only, three
history-only and nine neither. All 32 outputs were available; 24 had useful components but
failed strict requirements. The grader recorded interpretation sensitivities on 30 candidates.
Frozen judgments remain unchanged. History eliminated the six graded delivery gaps on this
panel, but did not improve the useful-output count or establish better reader reasoning.
Independent post-grading component/source audit confirms that all gold-required evidence IDs
and original bodies are delivered in all 16 history slots. This is evidence coverage, not
current authority or reader entailment. Eight candidates were classified primarily as
unsupported or stale status conclusions; ten candidates omitted actual event citations
entirely. No systematic hidden-inventory requirement or numeric citation quota was found.
Attribution and interpretation sensitivities remain disclosed without rescoring. The next
bounded hypothesis keeps full history, one call and citation requirements fixed, and tests an
explicit reader instruction preserving unknown, reported, proposed, current and historical status.

V5 remains 7/16 useful per reader; V6 remains direct 0/16 and staged 2/16. V4 remains compact
3/16, full 1/16 and working-context 0/16, strict 4/48. These are different fresh panels and do
not establish causal cross-version improvements. No failed case was removed or rerun.

The single hypothesis changed delivered evidence membership. Both arms used the unchanged
V5 concise reader, current query, one-string TextResult schema, ornith15 model, temperature 0,
seed 42, thinking disabled and one generation call. Fixed source columns include exact event
ID, source, actor, timestamp, session, revision status, parent IDs, eligible leaf IDs and body.
Current rows were the identical ordered active subset of the history packet. Inactive bodies
were historical evidence, without clause merging or promotion to current authority. Later
notes explicitly retain any older constraint required as current by the test contract.

Recovery used public, owner/project-scoped MemoryGate.evidence and a bounded original lifecycle
manifest plus single eligible-leaf closure, at most 16 records. Missing, forgotten, foreign,
future, forked, truncated, orphaned or inconsistent records fail the slot without pruning.
Full annotated objects and erasure generation are revalidated at current time before and after
generation, followed by the existing atomic store_turn evidence/generation comparison in a
fresh session. Query and agent-inferred answer alone persist. Each checkpoint uses a fresh
isolated lifecycle replay; generated answers never enter later source packets. No live memory,
production Python, model installation, service or security setting changed.

Independent preflight accepted 57 offline tests and Ruff checks. Actual gate/SQLite tests
prove inactive-basis success, scope and erasure isolation, changing leaves/forks, future-time
activation, full metadata comparisons and atomic rollback. Preflight fixture-identity and
operation-anchor findings were fixed before freeze. All four or 32 scheduled final slots are
preallocated, including setup failures. Whole-template input is capped at 3008 tokens, output
at 1024 with 64 reserve within 4096; actual full HTTP payload is capped at 49152 bytes.
Only transport failures can retry, at most three attempts with 2/8-second backoff.

Source, runtime, procedure and public inputs froze before public generation. The public
protocol persisted 4/4 outputs in 18.2774864 seconds: 4 chats, 8 counter and 1 metadata request,
13 HTTP total; 1835 input and 406 output tokens, including 692 cached input. Public citation,
status and drafting concerns were recorded without tuning. Persistence was a protocol pass.
Only then did a fresh method-isolated author create 8 stories and 16 checkpoints: 6 personal,
2 project, 4 English, 4 Russian, 40 events, 6 revision links and 2 unrelated scoped forgets.
Gold was never imported by runtime or sent to the model; root hashed its bytes without decoding.

A separate fresh grader saw frozen gold and randomized final artifacts with actual delivered
source/query packets, without arm names, prompts, cost, mapping or prior panels. Source
membership and style can still reveal methods; this is partial masking. All 32 judgments froze
before unmasking. No evaluator change silently rescored outputs.

The grader used a keyed judgment format permitted by the protocol. The original list-only
aggregation helper failed before exporting. A separately reviewed compatibility helper retained
all identity/pairing/scoring checks. Its first export miscounted delivery-gap objects by object
truthiness; a separately reviewed v3 helper uses the explicit boolean present flag. The flawed
v2 export is preserved as an accounting erratum; the corrected report is UNMASKED_GRADING_v3.json.
Strict scores, critical/citation/material counts, pairing and gate are identical. Original
12 frozen procedure files, original judgments and gold remain byte-identical. Synthetic helper
tests cover list/keyed formats, six true gap flags, duplicate identities/pairs and unknown arms.

Closed execution ended normally in 250.0761883 seconds. All 32 calls and finals succeeded at
the transport/schema/persistence level: 32 chats, 64 counter requests and 1 metadata request,
97 HTTP total. Raw physical receipts contain 18507 input and 8783 output tokens; 8726 cached
input is included, never subtracted. There were no retries or unknown usage. All 32 request
bodies matched exact recorded source packets and queries; all 16 pairs had identical columns,
scope, query and ordered current rows. Source/runtime/procedure hashes remained unchanged.
This proves fidelity of delivered packets, not entailment or useful task behavior.

Maintenance components are already included in slot time and must not be added again. One
model identity check is shared phase overhead, not arbitrarily charged twice. Every pair ran
current then history; cache state and server load were uncontrolled, so the lower observed
history latency does not establish a causal speed advantage. History used 553 more input tokens.
Public costs are separately charged. Hash storage made no LAN embedding calls; this is not an
external embedding efficiency estimate. Local CPU, energy, server load and total platform
grading cost were not measured.

bytes plus 5440 manifest bytes. A later independent all-output snapshot totals 26521990 bytes,
including wire receipts/reports/exports then present, and can grow. These are evaluation
footprints, not production archive sizes. Synthetic pre-forget snapshots remain retained;
scoped forgetting does not erase those snapshots.

The latest primary research remains a caution, not a replacement benchmark.
[Lapse](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.36457v1), September 29, separates temporal qualification
lost during writing from reader use and reports a degenerate planned downstream task.
[Stashbird](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.34242v1), September 28, reports prompt-token savings
with more retrieval calls; its update/deletion capabilities were outside the reported benchmark
evaluation. [After the Fix](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.34603v2), September 29, distinguishes
improvement over a failed predecessor from benefit over independent execution. None proves
Morgan's usefulness, erasure behavior or efficiency.

PR59 remains experimental, open, non-draft and unmerged. Exact frozen-source CI passed;
remote final Codex review remains pending as of this report. Product correctness and persistence
do not override the failed quality screen. Further work must diagnose reader versus evaluation
interpretation failures and test one bounded change with a fresh panel, preserving this result.
