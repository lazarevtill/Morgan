# Measured reader budget v2: valid development failure

The frozen evidence-closure plus `MemoryGate.inspect` candidate failed its development gate:
3/8 strictly useful answers or continuations, compared with raw retrieval 5/8 and recent
history 2/8. No heldout calls followed. This is a valid negative result, independently checked
for instrument integrity and graded before method identities were revealed. It does not
justify deploying the candidate for the owner's general life or projects.

| Method | Strictly useful / fixed denominator | Observed critical errors | Criticality unknown | Available deliveries |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recent same-scope history | 2/8 | 1 | 1 | 7/8 |
| Raw retrieval | 5/8 | 1 | 0 | 8/8 |
| Evidence closure + inspect | 3/8 | 0 | 0 | 8/8 |

The prospective gate required inspect at least 7/8, no critical errors or unknown criticality,
and usefulness no worse than both comparators. Zero observed inspect criticals on eight stories
is a limited observation, not a safety guarantee. All five inspect failures were safe abstentions
on endpoints requiring an answer or continuation. Raw retrieval retained one materially
unsupported historical assertion; recent history executed one wrong continuation stage. The
remaining recent-history failure timed out during token counting before any generation or effect.
No failure was retried, excluded, replaced or reinterpreted as success.

## Prospective design and independent admission

A new author created 24 disjoint synthetic owner/story clusters before outputs: 64 events over
32 sessions and at least 180 simulated days each, with 40 target-scope events, 16 same-owner
other-project events and eight other-owner events. Development used eight stories; the other
16 remained unrun. The domain/language/endpoint allocation was jointly balanced across personal
life and projects, English and Russian, answers, continuation, and abstention. Continuation
stages varied; some policies became effective after query cutoff. Independent semantic review
required substantive diverse histories, evolving threads, current-state evidence, scope and
permission consistency, and sufficient alternative proof sets. A rejected first pre-output
draft was retained; a corrected second draft passed and was frozen before quality calls.

The three methods used the same request and scope/cutoff. Recent history selected the latest
eight eligible same-scope events. Raw retrieval used eight seeds from one query; the candidate
expanded evidence pointers to at most 16 IDs over four rounds before actual inspect. Raw and
inspect shared their seed query. This tests a composite closure-plus-inspect method, not
inspect rendering alone. Sources used actual Morgan gates in fresh processes with deterministic
local lexical SHA1024 embeddings and no source-worker network/model calls. This is not the
production semantic embedding backend, consolidation, or real elapsed multi-day reliability.

One fixed already-loaded LAN reader (`ornith15`) used the same native template and policy for
all arms, with native whole-input ceiling 7,040 tokens, output ceiling 1,024, reserve 128,
16,384-byte packed context and 65,536-byte candidates. Packing kept whole units. The original
14,400-second campaign clock included preparation and grading and was not renewed. Each first
response had fixed timing bounds; the generation limit was 120 seconds. Physical requests and
first observed response chunks were durably retained, including interruption/failure paths.
The independent grader received all 24 opaque results and their raw/partial evidence with the
frozen rubric and only development gold. Grades were immutable before unblinding.

Eight phase metadata requests are additional to the arm HTTP counts. Total: 489 physical HTTP
attempts, 217 native token-count operations (each apply-template plus tokenize), and 23
physical generations with 23 known usage receipts: 61,422 input and 3,679 output tokens;
6,371 input tokens were reported cached. There are no unknown generation-usage attempts.
These are equal ceilings, not equal actual token, time, compute or monetary budgets.
Inspect used more input tokens and reader time than raw retrieval while scoring lower.

Shared source preparation used 264 processes (256 two-event stores and eight queries),
512 successful stores and 520 local embedding items/calls. Worker self elapsed time summed
to 13.45 seconds; source/frame parent elapsed time was 99.60 seconds including process startup
and orchestration. The full phase through export took 1,453.02 seconds. Driver CPU was
2.58 user plus 3.08 system seconds; owned children used 94.05 user plus 40.82 system seconds.
Those inclusive child totals contain source and reader CPU; do not add the component totals
again. Packing-only CPU was not separately measured. Known packing wall time was 14.40,
25.69 and 17.85 seconds for recent, raw and inspect respectively; the failed slot's missing
packing duration is excluded from those component sums, not from the fixed denominator.

Across 1,305 resource samples, reclaimable memory stayed at least 6,289,653,760 bytes;
maximum sampled child RSS was 66,600,960 bytes, and driver peak RSS through export was
74,432,512 bytes. The full v2 artifact tree was 57,272,758 bytes at independent audit, below
its 512 MiB cap. Resource observations are sampled, not continuous extrema. No user apps
were stopped. Monetary cost, generation-server CPU/GPU/energy/hardware cost and platform
research/author/reviewer/grader physical model calls and token use remain unknown. The JSON
summary preserves detailed measured retrieval CPU/wall, route times and scope caveats.

## Evidence and consequence

Independent exact-instrument admission, semantic preflight and postrun integrity/cost review
all passed. The development quality gate failed. The result changes no production defaults,
imports no real memory, and installs or integrates no other apps. Root and runtime did not
read semantic gold; isolation on the shared filesystem is procedural, not hardware enforced.
No gold, expected answers, source scenarios, private mapping or original caller text is
published here. Language compliance was annotated separately rather than added as a
post-output scoring gate; this result makes no broad bilingual-quality claim.
